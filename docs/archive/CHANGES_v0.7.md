# ECKY THUMP — v0.7 Artwork Integration

- Seven newly illustrated adult characters matched to existing roster IDs (no extra duplicate seats).
- 384px face-and-shoulders WebP portraits for selection and scoreboards.
- Seven optimised full-body images for TV and phone celebration panels.
- Match-winner and overall-champion artwork, with portrait fallback for the rest of the 32-character cast.

- Attribution appears only after the voting phase.
