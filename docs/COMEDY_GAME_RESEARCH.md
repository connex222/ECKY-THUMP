# ECKY THUMP — Party-Comedy Competitive Research (19 October 2026)

These are comparisons of **general mechanics**, not source text to reproduce.

| Game | Verified design lesson | What we do differently |
|---|---|---|
| Bad Cards | Player-made decks, adjustable adult content, easy private online access (https://bad.cards/ ; https://bad.cards/custom-packs/) | Keep invite-only play and original rating-tiered content; defer public pack publishing |
| Rotten Apples | Vote/judge settings, discarding poor hand cards and custom packs (https://bloob.io/rottenapples) | One private swap in each response-card round; retain democratic voting |
| Quiplash 3 | Short prompts, head-to-head creative replies, moderated user text, varied finale (https://www.jackboxgames.com/games/quiplash-3) | Every player gets an equal voice; Chaos Contracts change creative constraints |
| Survive the Internet | Context changes can make ordinary statements unexpectedly funny (https://www.jackbox.studio/games/survive-the-internet) | Preserve original seed secrecy and separate scoring for the rewriter; more starting situations |
| Tee K.O. | Fun emerges when unexpected creations are mixed and remixed (https://www.jackboxgames.com/games/tee-k-o) | Double Trouble combines an authored response with a player's invented pitch, without drawing |
| Fibbage 4 | Unbelievable claims and playful bluffs engage both writers and guessers (https://www-origin.jackboxgames.com/games/the-jackbox-party-pack-9/fibbage-4) | Consider a later deception mode rather than bolting new rules into v0.9 |
| Monikers | Reusing familiar material with altered constraints becomes funnier as players build inside jokes (Reddit community discussion: https://www.reddit.com/r/boardgames/comments/wnqgjy/) | Retain past prompt rotation; consider callbacks and optional bonus challenges later |
| Wavelength | Talking together and reacting is entertaining even before scoring (https://www.wavelength.zone/about) | Preserve relaxed host advancement to make space for banter |
| Snake Oil / Funemployed | Players enjoy pitching an absurd item or character to other players (community discussion: https://www.reddit.com/r/boardgames/comments/1fnoq2a/) | Deepen Double Trouble copy and eventually test a spoken sales-pitch option |
| Herd Mentality | Accessible prompts and group voting lower the barrier to entry (https://bigpotato.com/products/herd-mentality) | Keep rules simple: choose/write, then Gold and Silver vote |

## Implications

- **Best immediate value:** more varied prompts and responses, content rotation, and one meaningful tactical card choice.
- **Best future experiments:** opt-in custom private packs for friends, funny audience reaction buttons, head-to-head bonus duels, a two-part deception round, voice-acted cues, and an optional live pitch segment.
- **Not yet justified:** public matchmaking, user-generated public packs, AI joke generation for live games, paid subscriptions, complex moderator staffing.
- **Important limitation:** genre features can be adapted, but wording, art, branding, and unique expressive implementations must be original. Verify rights before any commercial sale.

## Five practical playtest questions

1. Which prompts made people laugh **before** any answer was submitted?
2. Did the group become more engaged during Card Clash, Quick Wit or Context Collapse?
3. Did participants understand the change in rules between rounds without needing the host to explain?
4. Did the one-card swap increase control without slowing the match?
5. Does a double-points finale make the champion feel earned, or does it make earlier rounds seem meaningless?
