# ECKY THUMP — Showtime Showdown v1.0

## What's new

- All five challenges use a stronger stage identity with large round cards, prompts, and voting frames.
- TV presentation has large stage titles, highlighted prompts, dedicated gold/silver voting and more dramatic final winner screen.
- Animated scene entrances and confetti for results and championship winners (respects reduced-motion).
- Original browser-generated show FX: selection, gold/silver voting, submit, new round, voting open, reveal, last-five-second tick and champion fanfare.
- Show sound effects are independently opt-in on **each** TV or phone; lobby music remains an independent control and does not auto-play.
- Sounds fire on phase transitions, not on every live state refresh. The finale awards exactly twice the ordinary rounded points.
- No new characters, external audio dependency, accounts, payments or game-rule changes.

## Games-night sound

Enable **SHOW SFX** on the main TV only if everyone is in the same room, to prevent multiple devices playing the same effects.
For multiple homes, each TV may enable effects locally. Browsers require a click before they permit sound.
